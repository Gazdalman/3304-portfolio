Top 5 Video Games

  1. Baldur's Gate 3

    A turn-based RPG set in the world of Dungeons & Dragons.

  2. Red Dead Redemption 2

    A Western action-adventure game set in the late 1800s created by Rockstar Games that follows arthur Morgan and Dutch's Gang.

  3. God of War: Ragnarok

    A mythological action-adventure game where Kratos and his son Atreus face the coming Ragnarök.

  4. Persona 5 Royal

    A role playing game where the player takes control of a group of students who become the Phantom Thieves.

  5. Bloodborne

    A dark fantasy action RPG where the player controls a hunter searching for a cure to a mysterious disease.
